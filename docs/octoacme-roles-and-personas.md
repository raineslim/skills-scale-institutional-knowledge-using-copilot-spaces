# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide the engineering approach, keep architecture aligned to business goals, and help the team deliver technically sound solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain technical direction and design principles
- Support architecture decisions and technical trade-offs
- Partner with Product and PM on feasibility, sequencing, and risk
- Mentor developers and help unblock technical decisions
- Ensure implementation quality aligns with acceptance criteria and non-functional requirements

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, scalable, and secure solutions
- Reduce avoidable technical rework and delivery risk
- Align engineering execution with product and project priorities

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review meetings and technical planning sessions
- Design docs and implementation discussions
- Escalation paths for technical blockers and dependency issues

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate priorities into feasible technical scope
- Coordinates with Project Managers on milestones, sequencing, and risk mitigation
- Supports Developers by clarifying standards, design decisions, and engineering constraints
- Collaborates with QA and Security partners to ensure release readiness and control compliance

---

## QA / Test Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads define quality standards, test strategy, and release readiness criteria so teams can deliver reliable, user-ready outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans, coverage goals, and acceptance validation steps
- Lead functional, regression, and integration test planning
- Partner with Developers and Product on quality gates and defect triage
- Provide release readiness recommendations and signal risk areas in changes
- Help identify gaps in validation for user-critical workflows

### Goals
- Catch defects early and reduce release risk
- Improve confidence in product quality and usability
- Ensure quality checks are visible, measurable, and repeatable

### Typical Communication
- Sprint quality reviews and defect triage
- Test planning and release readiness check-ins
- Feedback loops with PM, Product, and Engineering on quality trends

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to define testable acceptance criteria and regression coverage
- Aligns with Product Managers on what constitutes acceptable quality and user value
- Reports quality risks and readiness to Project Managers for milestone decisions
- Coordinates with Technical Leads on complex or cross-system validation needs

---

## Security / Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance partners identify risks, policy requirements, and control gaps so the team can deliver safely and meet organizational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review security, privacy, and compliance requirements for planned work
- Assess risk exposure and recommend mitigation or design controls
- Validate that delivery meets required policies, review gates, and regulatory expectations
- Support incident response and security escalation when issues arise
- Educate project stakeholders on security obligations and decision trade-offs

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance exposure
- Embed controls early rather than late in delivery
- Support secure and policy-aligned delivery without slowing value realization unnecessarily

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and security check-ins
- Risk assessments and policy validation discussions
- Escalation support during incidents or release blocking issues

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Technical Leads on secure architecture and risk-reduction options
- Advises Project Managers on compliance dependencies and mitigation timelines
- Supports Product Managers in prioritizing risk-aware delivery decisions
- Coordinates with QA to validate control-related requirements and release readiness

---

## Customer / User Representative

### Role Summary
Customer or User Representatives ensure delivery stays grounded in real user needs, usability, and business value.

### Responsibilities
- Share user needs, pain points, and context from the field or customer base
- Validate that proposed solutions address the actual problem and expected outcomes
- Provide feedback on usability, workflows, and adoption risks
- Participate in prioritization conversations and milestone reviews
- Help confirm when requirements are met from a customer perspective

### Goals
- Protect customer value and usability
- Improve product-market fit and adoption readiness
- Ensure stakeholder needs are understood and reflected in decisions

### Typical Communication
- Requirement reviews and user feedback sessions
- Milestone demos and acceptance discussions
- Product and roadmap alignment meetings

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides product context and feedback to Product Managers and PMs
- Helps Developers and QA understand real-world usage scenarios and edge cases
- Signals business-impacting issues that may influence scope or release timing
- Contributes to risk and dependency discussions with stakeholders and the project team

---

## Operations / Release Manager

### Role Summary
Operations and Release Managers coordinate deployment readiness, production support, and operational continuity so releases are predictable and recoverable.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release windows, deployment readiness, and rollback planning
- Support staging and production validation before go-live
- Document operational dependencies, monitoring needs, and post-release support expectations
- Help coordinate handoff between delivery teams and support or operations groups
- Monitor release outcomes and support incident or rollback decisions when needed

### Goals
- Reduce production disruption and deployment risk
- Improve reliability of release execution and operational readiness
- Enable fast, informed response when incidents or regressions occur

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment check-ins
- Post-release monitoring and incident communications
- Coordination with PMs, Technical Leads, and support teams

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Project Managers on milestone, launch timing, and operational dependencies
- Partners with Technical Leads on rollback plans and production risk review
- Coordinates with QA on release quality gates and validation evidence
- Supports Product Managers and stakeholders with communication around release impact and readiness

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles should be treated as complementary to the core project team, with clear accountability boundaries and handoff points across planning, delivery, testing, and release.
